Multiple Rule Conflict Strategy

What happens when a product matches more than one active Sale rule at the same time?

Example: a product is in both "Men" (20% off) and "Perfume" (40% off) categories.
🏆
Highest %
Best deal wins. Use the largest matching percentage.
Result: 40% off
🛡️
Lowest %
Most conservative. Use the smallest matching percentage.
Result: 20% off
📊
Average %
Arithmetic mean of all matching percentages.
Result: (20+40)÷2 = 30% off
➕
Sum % (max 100)
Add all percentages together, capped at 100%.
Result: 20+40 = 60% off
Changes apply immediately to all front-end price calculations.
